Instrument: IRS Form 8621 Instructions
Issuing authority: Internal Revenue Service
Provision: Section 4.2 — PFIC information return

Section 4.2 — PFIC information return
A United States person who is a shareholder of a Passive Foreign Investment
Company (PFIC) — including a foreign mutual fund, foreign ETF, or other
foreign pooled investment vehicle meeting the income or asset test under
Section 1297 of the Internal Revenue Code — must file Form 8621 for each
taxable year in which the shareholder receives a direct or indirect
distribution from the PFIC, recognizes gain on a direct or indirect
disposition of PFIC stock, or makes certain elections with respect to the
PFIC, in addition to any FBAR or Form 8938 filing otherwise required.
