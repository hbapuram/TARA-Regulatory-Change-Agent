Irish Residence Permit (IRP) — Registration & Renewal Guidance

Instrument: Immigration Service Delivery — Registering Your Immigration
Permission & Renewing Your Registration guidance
Issuing authority: Immigration Service Delivery (Department of Justice)
Provision: Section 1.1 — Initial registration

Section 1.0 — Application
This guidance applies to a non-EEA, non-Swiss and non-UK national who has
been granted permission to remain in the State. A national of an EEA member
state, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom is not required to register with
the Immigration Service Delivery (ISD) or hold an Irish Residence Permit
(IRP) under this guidance.

Section 1.1 — Initial registration
A non-EEA, non-Swiss, non-UK national who intends to remain in the State for
longer than 90 days must register their permission with the ISD and be
issued an Irish Residence Permit (IRP) within 90 days of arrival in the
State, subject to payment of the applicable registration fee.

Section 1.2 — Renewal
A registered non-EEA, non-Swiss, non-UK national whose IRP is approaching
its expiry date must renew their registration before the current permission
expires. ISD guidance permits a renewal application to be submitted up to 12
weeks (84 days) before the current permission's expiry date. Where an
application is submitted during this window, the renewed permission takes
effect from the current permission's expiry date, not from the date the
renewal application was made.

Section 1.3 — Change of address or personal circumstances
A registered non-EEA, non-Swiss, non-UK national must notify the ISD of any
change to their registered address, and of certain other changes in
personal circumstances, for as long as they hold a permission to remain in
the State. This guidance does not publish a fixed number of days within
which such a notification must be made.
