Tax and Duty Manual — Part 27-01A-02
Taxation of personal portfolio investment undertakings and offshore funds:
the eight-year deemed disposal rule

Instrument: Tax and Duty Manual Part 27-01A-02
Issuing authority: Office of the Revenue Commissioners
Provision: Section 4.2 — Deemed disposal at the eighth anniversary

Section 4.1 — Application
This section applies to a chargeable person who is Irish tax resident or ordinarily
resident and who holds an interest in a personal portfolio investment undertaking,
an offshore fund within the meaning of Chapter 4 of Part 27 TCA 1997, or a foreign
life assurance policy in a jurisdiction outside the State.

Section 4.2 — Deemed disposal at the eighth anniversary
Where an interest described in Section 4.1 has been held continuously for a period
of eight years, the holder is deemed to have disposed of, and immediately
reacquired, that interest on the eighth anniversary of its acquisition, whether or
not an actual disposal has occurred. The deemed disposal recurs on each subsequent
eighth anniversary for as long as the interest continues to be held.

Section 4.3 — Rate of charge
Tax arising on a deemed disposal under Section 4.2 is chargeable at the rate of
41 per cent of the deemed gain.

Section 4.4 — Return and payment obligation
A chargeable person to whom Section 4.2 applies must include the deemed gain in
their return of income for the chargeable period in which the eighth anniversary
falls, and must pay the tax due on or before the specified return date for that
chargeable period. Failure to include the deemed gain constitutes an incorrect
return.

Section 4.5 — Credit on actual disposal
Where an actual disposal of the interest subsequently occurs, credit is given for
tax already paid on any deemed disposal in respect of that interest, so as to
avoid a double charge on the same economic gain. The holder must retain
documentary evidence of the deemed disposal computation and the tax paid in order
to substantiate the credit claimed.
