FinCEN Form 114 Instructions & Form 8938 Instructions — Report of Foreign
Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) and Statement of Specified Foreign
Financial Assets

Instrument: FinCEN Form 114 Instructions (FBAR) & IRS Form 8938 Instructions (FATCA)
Issuing authority: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network / Internal Revenue Service
Provision: Section 3.2 — FBAR filing

Section 3.1 — Application
This guidance applies to a "United States person" — a citizen or resident of
the United States, regardless of where that person is domiciled or tax
resident under any other country's law. United States tax and reporting
obligations under this section attach to citizenship (or US residency) and
are not relieved by becoming tax resident of another country; they are
separate from, and can run alongside, that other country's own rules. A
person who is neither a US citizen nor a US resident is outside the scope of
this guidance.

Section 3.2 — FBAR filing
A United States person who has a financial interest in, or signature
authority over, one or more foreign financial accounts must file FinCEN Form
114 (the "FBAR") for any calendar year in which the aggregate value of those
accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time during the year.

Section 3.3 — FATCA specified foreign financial asset reporting
A specified individual within the scope of Section 3.1 who holds specified
foreign financial assets with an aggregate value above the applicable
reporting threshold must attach Form 8938 to their federal income tax return
for that year, in addition to, and not instead of, any FBAR filing required
under Section 3.2.
