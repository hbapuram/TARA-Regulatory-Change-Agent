Income Tax Return Instructions — Schedule FA (Foreign Assets) & Schedule FSI
(Foreign Source Income)

Instrument: Income Tax Return Instructions — Schedule FA & Schedule FSI
Issuing authority: Central Board of Direct Taxes
Provision: Section 2.2 — Schedule FA disclosure

Section 2.1 — Application
This guidance applies to an individual who is Resident and Ordinarily Resident
(ROR) in India for the relevant assessment year, as determined under Section 6
of the Income-tax Act, 1961. A Non-Resident (NR) or a Resident but Not
Ordinarily Resident (RNOR) individual is outside the scope of Schedule FA —
the foreign-asset disclosure obligation attaches to residential status, not to
citizenship, and does not apply to an Indian citizen who is tax resident
elsewhere.

Section 2.2 — Schedule FA disclosure
An individual within the scope of Section 2.1 who, at any time during the
relevant accounting period (1 January to 31 December preceding the assessment
year), holds a foreign asset — including a foreign bank account, foreign
equity or debt interest, foreign immovable property, or any other financial
interest in an entity located outside India — must disclose that asset in
Schedule FA of the return of income for the corresponding assessment year,
whether or not the asset produced any income during the period.

Section 2.3 — Schedule FSI disclosure
Income earned outside India by an individual within the scope of Section 2.1
must be reported in Schedule FSI of the return of income, together with
particulars of any foreign tax paid and any credit claimed against Indian tax
under an applicable double taxation avoidance agreement.

Section 2.4 — Beneficial ownership and beneficiary interests
The Schedule FA disclosure obligation under Section 2.2 extends to an
individual who holds a foreign asset as a beneficial owner or as a
beneficiary, and not only where legal title is held directly. An individual
within the scope of Section 2.1 who is a beneficial owner of, or a
beneficiary in, a foreign trust, foreign entity or foreign account must
disclose that interest in Schedule FA in the same manner as a directly-held
asset, whether or not any distribution was received during the period.
