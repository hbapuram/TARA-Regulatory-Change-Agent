Income-tax Rules, 1962 — Rule 128: Foreign Tax Credit

Instrument: Form 67 Instructions (Statement of Income from a Country or
Specified Territory Outside India and Foreign Tax Credit)
Issuing authority: Central Board of Direct Taxes
Provision: Section 5.1 — Form 67 claim

Section 5.1 — Form 67 claim
An individual claiming a credit against Indian tax for foreign tax paid or
deducted on income reported in Schedule FSI under an applicable double
taxation avoidance agreement must furnish Form 67, giving particulars of the
foreign income and the foreign tax charged on it, on or before the end of
the relevant assessment year, provided the return of income for that year
was itself filed within the time allowed under Section 139(1) or Section
139(4) of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
