IRS Form 3520 Instructions — Annual Return to Report Transactions With
Foreign Trusts and Receipt of Certain Foreign Gifts

Instrument: IRS Form 3520 Instructions
Issuing authority: Internal Revenue Service
Provision: Section 4.1 — Form 3520 reporting

Section 4.1 — Form 3520 reporting
A United States person who, during the taxable year, receives a distribution
from a foreign trust, is treated as the owner of any part of a foreign trust
under the grantor trust rules, or receives foreign gifts or bequests from a
nonresident alien individual or foreign estate exceeding the applicable
reporting threshold, must file Form 3520 for that taxable year, separately
from and in addition to any FBAR or Form 8938 filing otherwise required.
