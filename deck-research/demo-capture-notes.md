## What to capture for the pitch

1. **AI/MCP ready state** — The landing page shows that the default mode is **AI/MCP: on** and the status says **AI / MCP READY**. Maeve is the recommended prepared case.
2. **Visible AI orchestration trace** — Start Maeve. Capture the readable trace that shows the model discovering the permitted domain, source, and prepared holding through MCP. This is the proof that the AI is genuinely orchestrating tools.
3. **What changed** — Capture the simple Before / Now comparison: the relevant Revenue rate changed from 41% to 38% for the 2026 event.
4. **What applies** — Capture the four next steps. Use plain labels only; do not feature internal obligation IDs in the pitch frame.
5. **Evidence returned** — Capture the old 41% calculation and the plain-language “Needs correction” response. This is the strongest safe-failure visual.
6. **Evidence accepted** — Capture the restored 38% calculation and the “Accepted” response.
7. **Proof record** — Capture the completed source-to-action record and tamper-check result. Describe it as an inspectable proof record, not as an immutable legal filing.

## Canonical public claims

- **OpenAI** is the server-side orchestration layer in the default browser path.
- The model discovers and sequences **12 guarded MCP tools**; it does not own rates, dates, thresholds, arithmetic, or evidence outcomes.
- The browser has a labelled deterministic backup if the model or MCP subprocess fails, followed by a clearly labelled captured replay if the host is unavailable.
- **115 automated tests** and **8 executable acceptance checks** pass in the public release.
- **9 rule packs** are loaded. Maeve is the recommended end-to-end demonstration; the remaining packs demonstrate architecture breadth, not independent legal validation.
- The demo uses controlled, versioned source snapshots and prepared synthetic cases. It is not continuous current-law monitoring and does not handle real client data.
- A qualified human professional is required before real-world reliance or action.
